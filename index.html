Campus Improvement Initiative

Problems & Solutions

How the ParkFlow portal addresses key challenges faced by campus parking operations.

Problem #1

Rapidly Growing Student Population

As campus enrollment swells year-over-year, static garage space experiences severe bottlenecks during morning rush hours, causing traffic spillover onto surrounding city roads.

Our Portal Solution

Live level-by-level capacity telemetry balances garage distribution before drivers arrive, maximizing space utilization across upper floors and eliminating search delays without needing costly garage expansions.

Problem #2

Unchecked Rulebreakers & Poor Parking Habits

Drivers taking up multiple spots, blocking access lanes, or ignoring garage rules reduce usable capacity for everyone and create safety hazards for pedestrians.

Our Portal Solution

Enforces accountability with automated citation reporting, transparent dispute tracking, and swift penalty escalation for repeat offenders to keep parking orderly.

Problem #3

Unauthorized Outsiders & Non-Student Parking

Nearby commuters and unauthorized visitors frequently exploit open garage gates to park for free, depriving tuition-paying students of designated spaces.

Our Portal Solution

Synchronizes vehicle plate registrations with automated entry barriers, guaranteeing garage access is strictly restricted to valid student permit holders.
